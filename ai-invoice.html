How it works, for the judges: the seller describes a sale in plain Indonesian or English. An AI call (Pollinations text API, keyless) parses it into structured line items, which render as an editable invoice. PayPal Buttons create and capture a real sandbox order for the invoice total (IDR converted at a fixed, clearly shown demo rate of 16,000 IDR = 1 USD). The AI also drafts the WhatsApp payment reminder, and invoices can be saved locally or shared as a self-contained link.
